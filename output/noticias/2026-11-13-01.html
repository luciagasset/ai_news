Google ha anunciado este mes que los colegios podrán desactivar las funciones de IA para sus alumnos, con una excepción. Si no quieren los AI Overviews, los resúmenes de IA que aparecen sobre los resultados, perderán el buscador entero. La medida llega cuando los distritos escolares de Los Ángeles, Seattle y Nueva York acaban de prohibir la IA en clase. El alcalde de Nueva York, Zohran Mamdani, acusa a Google de usar su poder de monopolio para imponerla. Colegios de Chicago responden que prefieren quedarse sin buscador.

Google se remite a su anuncio, donde dice que quiere dar a los colegios control para usar la IA como crean conveniente. Tracy Pizzo, exempleada de Google, cree que el motivo es legal. Un juez rechazó hace poco unas demandas antimonopolio de editores al considerar que el buscador y la IA son un solo producto. Un interruptor solo para colegios demostraría que se pueden separar. Los expertos consultados por NPR no están del todo convencidos, pero no lo descartan.

Google announced this month that schools will be able to turn off AI features for their students, with one exception. If they do not want AI Overviews, the AI summaries that appear above the results, they will lose the search engine entirely. The move comes just as the school districts of Los Angeles, Seattle and New York have banned AI in class. New York Mayor Zohran Mamdani accuses Google of using its monopoly power to force it in. Chicago schools reply that they would rather go without the search engine.

Google points to its announcement, which says it wants to give schools control to use AI as they see fit. Tracy Pizzo, a former Google employee, believes the reason is legal. A judge recently dismissed antitrust lawsuits from publishers after ruling that Search and AI are a single product. An off switch for schools alone would show that they can be separated. The experts NPR consulted are not fully convinced, but do not rule it out.
