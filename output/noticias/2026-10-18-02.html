Jiaru Zou, James Zou and six other authors published “Hybrid Reasoning Agents” on arXiv (2610.18861) on October 13, with code on Together Computer's GitHub and models on Hugging Face. They start from a problem: current LLM agents reason and interact through text, while latent reasoning is a more efficient internal medium but lacks native tool access and transparency. Their proposal, HybridAgents, dynamically switches between latent reasoning and text-based interaction within a single generation process. A lightweight component, HybridLink, enables transitions between the two modes, and a continuous working memory preserves lossless hybrid context across multiple turns; according to the authors, this keeps tool-use capabilities and makes latent thoughts directly inspectable.

Post-training has two stages: HybridSFT stabilizes tool-integrated hybrid generation and HybridRL teaches the model when to think in latent space and when to act through text. They evaluate it at 3 model scales across 11 benchmarks in mathematics, science, medicine, search and agentic reasoning. Compared with frontier LLM agents and text-based and latent agentic frameworks, the authors claim an average improvement of 10.4%, 35.5% to 54.7% fewer tokens, 1.8x to 2.6x faster inference and 2.7x to 3.7x lower cost. These are the paper's own results, with no independent review in the sources.
